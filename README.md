### Marion Moranetz
San Francisco

Four things I found by measuring what usually gets argued about.

**15 of the 39 most-taught persuasion techniques have no peer-reviewed study behind them.**
11,785 records screened across 7 databases, 44 extracted. The protocol and the deviation log were committed before the results, so the work can be checked against what I said I would do.
[closing-evidence-atlas](https://github.com/Moranetz/closing-evidence-atlas)

**A colour that clears WCAG 4.5:1 against a dark ground lands at APCA Lc 35, under the floor for text at any size.**
The standard false-passes dark interfaces. One file, no dependencies, and `--selftest` reproduces four published reference values before you trust a number out of it.
[apca-gate](https://github.com/Moranetz/apca-gate)

**A citation pass on my own taxonomy corrected three of my errors and pulled two citations that do not resolve to any real publication.**
Assessment 001 carries two adverse findings and no composite score, because only one of six criteria was settleable from public evidence.
[cognitive-attack-surface](https://github.com/Moranetz/cognitive-attack-surface)

**I pre-registered a four-arm trial on my own cold-outreach openers and published the result regardless of which way it went.**
400 decisions, 12 weeks.
[sales-instrument](https://github.com/Moranetz/sales-instrument)

### Other instruments

- [mechanism-detector](https://github.com/Moranetz/mechanism-detector) — names which psychological mechanism an AI conversation is running, turn by turn. Labels manipulation and has no generation endpoint.
- [pam1-bandit](https://github.com/Moranetz/pam1-bandit) — a fruit-fly mushroom-body model playing a slot machine, and a sweep for which payout schedule hooks it hardest.
- [lsm-discovery](https://github.com/Moranetz/lsm-discovery) — a Python replication of Pennebaker's Language Style Matching, validated on persuasive dialogue.
- [clearframe-public](https://github.com/Moranetz/clearframe-public) — names the persuasion techniques in a headline and steelmans both sides without ideology labels.
